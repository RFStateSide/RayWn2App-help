Envelope Spectrum and modulation bands

On this page

  • View the envelope spectrum
  • Make a useful comparison
  • Band readings
  • Pause and save

View the envelope spectrum

Choose Capture → Envelope Spectrum. Select the WN-2 and sensor, then Start. The graph shows frequency components of the captured envelope. It is not a spectrum analyzer for the RF carrier frequency.


Envelope Spectrum of a simulated 500 Hz envelope. The nearest peak bin is about 508 Hz; this is not the RF carrier. Select image to enlarge.

Make a useful comparison

Choose the desired window and averaging options, then allow new captures to arrive. These choices affect how the trace is presented. Keep them consistent when comparing two operating conditions. Read the frequency and magnitude units on the axes. Peaks identify stronger components in the displayed envelope. Frequency spacing is limited by the capture length; zooming makes the graph larger but does not add measurement resolution.

Band readings

Where band controls are shown, set the frequency ranges of interest and read the corresponding band results. Use them to compare portions of the envelope spectrum under consistent settings. They are not direct measurements of RF emissions outside the transmitter's band.

Pause and save

Pause holds the displayed result for inspection. Reset Zoom returns to the wider view. Use Title for a meaningful plot name and Export PNG for an image. Check source status and Scope clipping before drawing conclusions from an old or distorted trace. The Tone Generator can coordinate a limited capture sequence with an audio stimulus; see Tone Generator.

Practice without transmitting

Open the WN-2 Raw Data Emulator, connect its identity in the main device list, and enable its Scope source. Set the envelope frequency to 500 Hz. Select that same emulator in this viewer and press Start. The illustration uses the nominal 40 ksample/s model. Simulated captures are identified on the axes and status line.
