Tone generator, pulses and analyzer coordination

On this page

  • Play a tone
  • Pulsed and low-duty-cycle operation
  • Coordinate with an analyzer
  • Other audio tools

Play a tone

Open Tools → Tone Generator. Select the audio output connected to your intended listening or radio-audio path. Set Tone #1 and Tone #2 frequencies, durations and amplitude. Choose Tone #1, Tone #2 or Both. Stop ends playback.


Tone Generator controls audio playback; it does not key your transmitter. Select image to enlarge.

Begin at a modest amplitude and use the station's normal audio-level checks. The output selection is an audio device on the computer, not a WN-2 channel. Changing devices or stopping the application ends the audio operation.

Pulsed and low-duty-cycle operation

Choose the pulsed option and set the on/off timing needed for your test. The duty cycle describes how much of each cycle contains tone. Check timing and duration before starting. A low duty cycle reduces average audio activity; it does not guarantee a safe RF power level.

Coordinate with an analyzer

Use the analyzer-coordination controls to choose the connected WN-2, sensor and Scope or Envelope Spectrum. Start the coordinated operation to play the selected stimulus and collect the specified short capture sequence. The application does not turn on RF. Establish the intended radio/audio path yourself. Captures are not precisely synchronized to each pulse edge, so do not interpret them as a triggered RF burst test.

Other audio tools

Tone Generator, Audible RF Monitor and Audio Messages coordinate so competing application audio operations do not keep playing over one another. Use Stop if you are uncertain which operation is active.
